Hephaestus

Hector Sanchez's private workshop tool

Hephaestus is a personal tool that runs on Hector's own Mac. It helps him manage his own YouTube channel (uploading and editing his videos, reading his channel's analytics) and his own Google Drive files.

It is not a public service: it has no sign-ups, and the only Google account it signs into is Hector's.
