Hephaestus Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Hephaestus is a personal tool that Hector Sanchez runs on his own Mac. It is not offered to the public, and the only Google account it signs into is his own. This page explains what it does with the Google data it can reach.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: his own channel's videos, playlists, comments, captions and settings, and his channel's analytics (views, watch time, audience and similar reports).
  • Google Drive: his own files, to move media in and out of the folders he chooses.

How it uses that data

Only to do what Hector asks for: upload and edit his videos, read his analytics and draw charts of them, and move his files. Nothing changes on his channel or goes public without his say-so.

Where the data goes

  • The sign-in keys and the data Hephaestus reads stay on Hector's Mac.
  • Hephaestus is run with an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude). Data needed for one of Hector's requests, such as the analytics behind a chart, is processed by it to carry out that request.
  • The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google's rules

Hephaestus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The sign-in keys can then be deleted from the Mac.
